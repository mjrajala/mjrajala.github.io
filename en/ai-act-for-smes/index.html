AI Act

The AI Act does not ban AI. It forces companies to manage it.

For an SME, the practical first step is knowing where AI is used, for what purpose, with what data, and under whose responsibility.

Request an AI inventoryAI agent guide

Management checklist

  • List AI tools and agents in use.
  • Record purpose, users, data, and decision rights.
  • Identify higher-risk domains such as HR or critical operations.
  • Define human review and keep logs for later audits.
