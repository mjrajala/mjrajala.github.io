Automation

AI automation for SMEs starts with one workflow.

Good automation does not try to replace the whole organization at once. It defines one repeated, measurable, and reviewable workflow where the benefit can be proven in practice.

Suggest a workflowSee TasaPay

A good target

The work has a clear start, a clear output, plenty of copying or comparison, and a result that can be reviewed before it affects customers, money, or statutory reporting.

1. Discovery

Identify bottlenecks, data, systems, and approval limits. If the process is unclear to humans, it must be clarified before automation.

2. Pilot

Build a small production-ready automation with logs, error limits, and a result that can be checked manually.

3. Scale

Expand only when the benefit is visible in metrics: saved time, better quality, faster throughput, or reduced compliance risk.

Typical use cases

  • Pre-processing requests for proposal and drafting replies.
  • Compiling reports from several systems.
  • Customer-service back office work and internal search.
  • Collecting compliance documents and listing missing items.

What we do not recommend

Do not start with an open-ended AI strategy if no concrete workflow has been selected. First define one measurable use case, an owner, and success criteria.
