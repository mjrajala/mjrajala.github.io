How it works

You ask and approve. Studio does the work in between.

Write the request the way you would to a colleague. Studio picks the right skill for the task itself, so there is no tool or template to hunt for.

Finished work shows up as a draft first. You can edit it, ask for another take or approve it. Approved posts go out on schedule, and reach and engagement collect in the same view.

Get started

Files

Ask for a file, get a file

Presentations, documents, spreadsheets and offers come out of the conversation as files you can download. Studio writes them with your business details and in your voice, so you are not filling in a blank template by hand.

When Studio learns something about your business, such as new prices, it is saved as a memory. A memory can be private or shared with the team, and you can delete it at any time.

Publishing

Write once, publish to four channels

Write the post yourself or ask Studio to write it. Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X each get their own take: one draft becomes a version in the right length and tone for each channel, and every version waits for your approval before it goes out.

Schedule a post or publish it right away. Earlier posts can be imported, so reach and engagement sit side by side in one view.

The whole week at a glance

The content calendar shows posts by day and by channel, from draft to published.

Approval

Nothing goes out without your approval

Posts, website fixes and files collect in the Inbox and wait for your decision. Once you trust the results, you can switch on autopilot.

Share a draft by link, comments land in the text

A draft can be shared as a link, with no account to create. The reader selects a passage and writes the comment right there, and can edit the text when you allow it.

Guests give a name as they comment, so you can see who asked for what. The link has an expiry time, and the feedback stays out of your email threads.

Website

Your website keeps getting better after launch

Studio scores your site on SEO, content and AI visibility. Every finding comes with a ready fix that you approve or dismiss.

Competitor tracking

Traffic analytics
