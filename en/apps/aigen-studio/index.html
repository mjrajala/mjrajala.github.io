Three steps, and two of them are yours

  1. 1

    Ask in your own words

    Type what you need. Studio picks the right skill for the task itself, so you never choose a tool or a template.

  2. 2

    Read the draft and approve

    The work is shown to you before it goes anywhere. You can edit it or ask for another take.

  3. 3

    Publishing and results

    Approved posts go out on schedule, and reach and engagement collect in the same view.

Content calendar

Content, approval and publishing in one place

A whole week of posts by channel, from draft to published.

One draft, every channel

Write the post yourself or ask Studio to write it. Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X each get their own version, scheduled or published right away.

Every draft comes to you for approval. Earlier posts can be imported, so reach and engagement sit side by side in one view.

Ask for a file, get a file

Presentations, documents, spreadsheets and offers come out of the conversation as files you can download. Studio writes them using your business details.

What Studio learns about your business is saved as memories. A memory can be private or shared with the team, and you can delete it at any time.

Website, results and team

Studio does not stop at publishing. It also watches what happens on your site and in the numbers.

Website review
Studio scores SEO, content and AI visibility. Every finding comes with a ready fix for you to approve.
Competitor tracking
A comparison shows where competitors are ahead of you on content and visibility.
Traffic analytics
Site traffic sits in the same view as the results of your posts.
Team and roles
The whole team can work in the same workspace with their own roles.
You decide
Approve changes yourself, or switch on autopilot once you trust the results. The action log shows what was done.
