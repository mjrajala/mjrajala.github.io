Before your first draft

What kinds of text can I write?

Newsletters, emails, proposals, reports, job ads, policies and social posts, for example. Start from a blank page or bring an existing draft for review.

Does Draftpad publish the finished text?

No. Copy the approved text into your email tool, website or other service yourself. Draftpad does not automatically publish or send content.

What is included for free?

Free includes one person and one workspace, unlimited drafts, versions and comments, a small AI allowance and versions for eight channels. No payment card is needed.

How is Team pricing calculated?

The first 20 people cost €5 each per month. Additional people from the 21st seat cost €4 each. For example, 25 people cost €120 and 50 cost €220 per month. VAT is added.

How does the team AI allowance work?

Team includes €1 of AI use per person each month. The allowance is pooled across the whole team.

Do I need my own AI agent?

No. Write and review on your own or with colleagues. Connecting your own agents is optional.
