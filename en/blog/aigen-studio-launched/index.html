Aigen Studio is live: documents, websites and publishing in one workspace

You ask in your own words, Studio drafts the work, and nothing goes out before you approve it.

An offer gets written in one tool, website copy in another and social posts in a third. Each one needs the same explanation of what the business does and who the text is for. Aigen Studio, opened today, brings that work into one AI workspace.

The pattern is the same whatever the task: you ask in your own words, Studio drafts the work, and you approve it. The service runs in English and Finnish.

One draft becomes a version per channel

Write the post yourself or ask Studio to write it. Each channel gets its own version at a length and tone that suits it, and you can ask for an image to go with it.

Drafts come to you for approval before anything is published, and an approved post can be scheduled or sent right away. Results start arriving within a day, and earlier posts can be imported so reach and engagement sit in one view.

A draft can be shared by link for comments

When the text needs a look from a client or a colleague first, you can share it as a link. There is no account to create: the reader selects a passage, writes the comment right there, and can edit the text when you allow it. Guests give a name as they comment, and the link carries an expiry time.

Content calendar

A week at a time, by channel

Each day lists post titles and channel labels. The view switches between week, work week, month and year.

Ask for a file, get a file

Presentations, documents, spreadsheets and offers come out of the same conversation as files you can download. Studio picks the specialist skill for the task itself, so there is no tool or template to choose.

What Studio learns about the business is stored as memories. A memory can be private or shared with the team, and it can be deleted. The next task then starts from what Studio already knows.

The website gets scored, the fix comes back for approval

Studio reviews your site's SEO, content and AI visibility. Beyond the score, every finding comes with a ready fix that you can approve or leave. Competitor tracking shows where others are ahead on content and visibility.

Autopilot can be switched on once you trust the system. The action log records what was done.

What the subscription costs

The launch offer is €29/month + VAT, and the rate stays the same while the subscription is active. The standard price later is €99/month + VAT. There is one plan, so every feature is included, together with a monthly usage allowance for AI work. The whole team can work in the same workspace with their own roles.
