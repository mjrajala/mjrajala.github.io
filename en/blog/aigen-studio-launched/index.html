Aigen Studio is live: documents, websites and publishing in one workspace

Aigen Studio brings small-business documents, website improvements, social publishing and results into one workspace in English and Finnish.

A small business may use separate tools for an offer, a client presentation and a website update. Each task starts with another explanation of the business and its customers. The newly launched Aigen Studio brings these jobs into one AI workspace.

Studio is built for everyday work in small businesses. It creates downloadable files, helps improve a website, writes and schedules posts, and reports on results. It works in English and Finnish.

From an offer to a presentation or spreadsheet

Studio creates presentations, documents, spreadsheets and offers you can download and use. You could prepare a client offer, then use the same business context to build a presentation. Studio automatically applies specialist skills suited to the task.

You can save business context as memories, keep them private or share them with a team, and delete them when needed. This gives the workspace background information for the next task.

A website review with proposed fixes

Studio scores website health, SEO, content and AI visibility. Alongside the assessment, it drafts improvements for approval. Competitor tracking adds other businesses’ websites to the comparison.

Start with a service page: look at its content assessment, review Studio’s proposed fix and approve the change when it accurately describes your service.

Posts for each channel, results in one place

Studio writes and schedules posts for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X. Drafts come to you for approval before publishing. You can create a version for each channel, adapting the same subject to the place where it will appear.

You can also import earlier posts. Reach and engagement analytics help you assess which content worked and what to change next time.

Work on your own or with a team

Teams and roles support shared work. Website changes are drafted for approval, and autopilot is optional. The action log records what was done. For example, a team can prepare drafts for each channel, then schedule them after approval.
