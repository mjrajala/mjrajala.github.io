I tendered insurance from five companies and did not open a single offer myself

A practical example of how an AI agent turned five insurance offers and policy terms into one clear comparison.

I tendered insurance from five companies and did not open a single offer myself.

Anyone who has compared insurance offers knows the swamp: five offers, five formats, and somewhere between the lines is the answer to which one is actually best. Usually that burns an evening. This time it did not.

I handed the offers to an AI agent and asked it to work out what I was really paying for.

The agent fetched each provider's current policy terms, compared coverage, deductibles and limitations, and marked the places where the lowest price meant weaker protection in practice. The result was one clear spreadsheet where the differences were visible at a glance.

I did not even have to go looking for it. The finished comparison came straight to WhatsApp and email.

It took a few minutes instead of a full evening wrestling with PDF files. Honestly, the result was more careful than what I would have had the patience to do myself.

This is the point: most teams do not need AI to do spectacular things. They need it to handle the dull, time-consuming work nobody wants to do: collecting information, comparing options and assembling a report.

What is the boring routine in your team that you would gladly hand over to a machine?
