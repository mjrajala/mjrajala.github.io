CBAM

CBAM reporting only works when data and responsibilities are under control.

CBAM reporting depends on import, supplier, product, and emissions data. The process should be built before every reporting period becomes a manual chase.

Ask about rolloutView CBAM Tool

Core data needed

  • Imported goods and CN codes.
  • Countries of origin and suppliers.
  • Product-specific emissions data.
  • Owners and deadlines for missing data.

The process must be repeatable

  • One report can be assembled by hand. The trouble starts when the same work repeats with the same suppliers, different gaps, and changing data. CBAM Tool turns that into a tracked workflow.

Management question

  • If customs data, purchasing data, and supplier emissions data do not match, reporting becomes hard to control. Process, responsibilities, and missing-data tracking should be in place before deadlines bite.
