CBAM

CBAM reporting only works when data and responsibilities are under control.

The CBAM transitional phase ran from 2023 to 2025, and the definitive phase began on 1 January 2026. Declarations in the definitive phase are annual. Collect import and emissions data throughout the year so it is ready before the deadline.

See CBAM ToolAsk about rollout

Core data needed

  • Imported products and CN codes.
  • Countries of origin and suppliers.
  • Product-level emissions data.
  • Responsibilities and deadlines for missing data.

The process must be repeatable

One report can be assembled by hand. The problem starts when the same work must be done again with the same suppliers, different gaps, and changing data. CBAM Tool turns this into a trackable workflow.

Management question

If customs data, purchasing data, and supplier emissions information do not connect, reporting quickly becomes hard to control. That is why process, responsibilities, and missing-data tracking should be built before deadlines.
