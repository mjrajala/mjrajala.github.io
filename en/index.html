What is an AI agent — and how is it different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent takes action.

An AI agent connects to your existing tools — your CRM, inbox, calendar, or database — and completes tasks on its own: booking appointments, updating records, sending follow-ups, or routing support tickets. It runs around the clock without needing someone to press a button each time.

At AI Generation, we build AI agents for business that handle specific, repeatable workflows from start to finish. You define the rules; the agent does the work.

What Finnish businesses use AI agents for

  • Customer service triage — the agent reads incoming messages, answers common questions, and escalates complex issues to a human.
  • Back-office data entry — the agent pulls information from emails or forms and updates your system automatically, removing manual copy-paste work.
  • Lead qualification — the agent contacts new enquiries, asks qualifying questions, and books a meeting in your calendar when the lead is ready.
  • Compliance reporting — the agent collects the required data and generates draft reports for EU regulations such as the AI Act or pay-transparency rules.

Every agent we build is scoped, tested, and handed over with clear documentation so your team stays in control.
