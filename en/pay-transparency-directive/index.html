Pay transparency

The Pay Transparency Directive requires data, not panic meetings.

The national implementation deadline for the EU Pay Transparency Directive is 7 June 2026. For a company, the practical question is: will pay data, comparison groups, and a reporting process be ready in time?

See TasaPayAsk about preparation

What should start now?

  • Pay data sources and quality.
  • Role definitions and work of equal value.
  • Pay gap calculation and documentation of explanations.
  • Management reporting view and action list.

Typical problem

Pay data lives in HR systems, finance, managers notes, and old spreadsheets. For the directive, that is not a process. It is an archaeological dig.

TasaPay role

TasaPay brings pay and personnel data into analyzable form, helps identify relevant pay gaps, and makes the reporting structure consistent.
