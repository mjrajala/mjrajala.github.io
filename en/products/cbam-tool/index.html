Turn CBAM reporting into a repeatable process.

CBAM Tool helps bring import, supplier, and emissions data into one place so reporting does not depend on scattered files, emails, and individual memory.

What does CBAM Tool help with?

  • Managing supplier- and product-specific information.
  • Tracking missing emissions data and reporting risks.
  • Building a repeatable reporting process.

Who is the solution for?

CBAM Tool fits companies that import goods covered by CBAM and need a practical way to manage reporting data, responsibilities, and gaps before deadlines.

Import and procurement

Tracks information needed from suppliers and identifies gaps early.

Finance and compliance

Collects required reporting data into a structured whole.

Management

Gets visibility into preparation status and reporting risks.

Why does CBAM break down in practice?

Reporting requires import, product, supplier, and emissions data that usually do not live in the same system. Without a clear process, every reporting period starts with searching and manual checking.

How does CBAM Tool help?

  • Collects supplier- and product-specific information in one place.
  • Tracks missing emissions data and open tasks.
  • Turns reporting into a repeatable process.
  • Reduces last-minute manual compilation.

Frequently asked questions

Who is CBAM Tool for?

For companies that import CBAM-covered goods or manage supplier and emissions data.

Does the tool solve emissions calculation?

The tool helps collect and manage the data required for reporting. The actual calculation basis must be defined according to the company situation.

Why start before the permanent phase?

Because supplier data, responsibilities, and review processes take time. Preparation should be done before reporting becomes operational pressure.
