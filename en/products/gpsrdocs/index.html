Regulation (EU) 2023/988

Product safety documentation in one managed view.

GPSRdocs helps collect risk assessments, product information, attachments, and owners in one place so GPSR-related documentation remains reviewable and up to date.

What does GPSRdocs help with?

  • Collecting product-specific documentation.
  • Tracking risk assessments, attachments, and gaps.
  • Sharing documentation status with management and the team.

Who is the solution for?

GPSRdocs fits companies that manufacture, import, or sell consumer products and need a clear way to manage product safety documents and gaps.

Product owners

See product-specific documents, risks, and open tasks in one view.

Compliance

Can track documentation coverage and missing attachments systematically.

Management

Gets the big picture of which product groups are ready and where work remains.

Why should documentation be centralized?

Product safety documentation usually consists of several materials: risk assessments, product information, supplier documents, instructions, and traceability. If information is scattered, gaps often appear only when someone asks for the material.

How does GPSRdocs help?

  • Collects product-specific documents into one view.
  • Helps identify missing attachments and open risks.
  • Makes documentation status visible to management.
  • Reduces dependency on individual folder structures.
