Directive (EU) 2019/1937

Internal reporting channel with clear handling.

Ilmoita helps an organization receive, track, and handle misconduct reports in a controlled way. The goal is to make the reporting process confidential, documented, and easy to roll out.

Open product Read guide

What does Ilmoita help with?

  • Receiving confidential reports.
  • Managing responsibilities and status.
  • Documenting and tracking cases in one place.

Who is the solution for?

Ilmoita fits organizations that need a practical internal reporting channel without a heavy rollout project. The focus is a clear process, confidentiality, and traceability.

Management

Gets visibility that reports are handled according to the agreed process.

HR and compliance

Can manage intake, handling, and documentation in one place.

Personnel

Gets a clear and confidential channel for reporting concerns.

Why is email not enough?

An internal reporting channel needs confidentiality, handling responsibilities, timestamps, and case tracking. A plain mailbox easily becomes person-dependent and hard to audit.

How does Ilmoita help?

  • Provides a clear channel for submitting reports.
  • Keeps handling in one system.
  • Reduces unnecessary spreading of personal data.
  • Supports lightweight rollout also in smaller organizations.

Frequently asked questions

The purpose of the solution is to support safe and confidential reporting according to the process defined by the organization.

Who is this for?

For organizations that need a controlled internal reporting channel and a clear handling process.

A report does not remain in one person's inbox. Handling can be documented and tracked systematically.
