What does Ilmoo help with?

  • Receiving confidential reports.

Who is the solution for?

Ilmoo fits organizations that need a practical internal reporting channel. The focus is a clear process, confidentiality, and traceability.

Management

Gets visibility that reports are handled according to the agreed process.

HR and compliance

Can manage intake, handling, and documentation in one place.

Personnel

Gets a clear and confidential channel for reporting concerns.

Why is email not enough?

An internal reporting channel needs confidentiality, handling responsibilities, timestamps, and case tracking. A plain mailbox easily becomes person-dependent and hard to audit.

How does Ilmoo help?

  • Provides a clear channel for submitting reports.
  • Keeps handling in one system.
  • Reduces unnecessary spreading of personal data.
