Whistleblowing

A whistleblowing channel is a process, not just a form.

A good reporting channel protects the reporter, guides the handler, and leaves the organization with a clear record of what happened and when.

See IlmoitaAsk about rollout

Basic requirements

  • Confidential reporting.
  • Clear handling responsibilities.
  • Case-specific tracking and deadlines.
  • Documentation without unnecessary spreading of personal data.

Why email is not enough

Email scatters reports into personal inboxes, makes responsibilities unclear, and complicates later audit. It works until the first genuinely difficult case.

Ilmoita product role

Ilmoita brings the report, handling, status, and documentation into one channel. The goal is a lightweight rollout without the weight of an enterprise system.
