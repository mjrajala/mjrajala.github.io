Whistleblowing

A whistleblowing channel is a process, not just a form.

A good reporting channel protects the reporter, guides the handler, and leaves a clear record of what happened and when.

Ask about rolloutView Ilmoita

Basic requirements

  • Confidential reporting.
  • Clear handling responsibilities.
  • Case tracking and deadlines.
  • Documentation without unnecessary personal data spread.

Why email is not enough

  • Email scatters reports into personal inboxes, blurs responsibilities, and makes later auditing painful. It works until the first genuinely difficult case.

Ilmoita role

  • Ilmoita brings the report, handling, status, and documentation into one channel. The goal is a lightweight rollout without enterprise-system bloat.
