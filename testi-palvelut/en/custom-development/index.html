How we deliver

One bounded workflow at a time.

We choose one bounded workflow, agree on the tools, limits and review points, and test the implementation with real data before expanding. That way the automation doesn’t stay a demo; it becomes part of everyday production work.

  1. DataDocuments, spreadsheets, systems
  2. AgentFinds, combines and drafts
  3. HumanReviews exceptions and approves
  4. DoneResult and a log of what was done

Good first targets

  • Compiling reports from several sources
  • Pre-processing requests for quotation and documents
  • Background work for customer service
  • Recurring compliance documentation

Decide before building

  1. Which workflow to automate first.
  2. Where the data comes from and who owns the outcome.
  3. Where human approval is needed.
  4. How the benefit is measured: time, quality, throughput or reduced risk.
