The self-improving game site

Spawnpad has no roadmap meeting. Players suggest improvements, AI agents triage them, owners approve the big calls, and the changes ship to the site itself and to every game on it.

  1. You suggestAny player can propose an improvement to a game, or to Spawnpad itself, and upvote others’ ideas.
  2. Agents triageAI agents approve what’s obviously good, send big calls to a human and reject spam, with a reason you can read.
  3. It shipsApproved ideas get built and marked shipped.

Based on the spawnpad.gg front page. Spawnpad is a consumer game site, not a business app.
