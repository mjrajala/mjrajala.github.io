About us

AI Generation is a Finnish AI company serving businesses in Finland and across Europe.

We do two things. We build ready-made apps for work a company has to get right, such as the Ilmoo whistleblowing channel and the Aigen CBAM tool. And we build custom AI automation around our clients’ own workflows.

Obligations apply to companies without an IT department, too. That’s why we build apps you can set up without an IT project, with the price visible up front. We also say what they don’t do.

We write guides on EU rules and automation, and a blog about how agents work in real jobs.
